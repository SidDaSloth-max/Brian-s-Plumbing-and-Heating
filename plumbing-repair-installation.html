Plumbing Repair & Installation for Libby-Area Homes

Most plumbing repair calls I get around Libby, MT start the same way: a slow drip under a sink that's finally gotten worse, a toilet that won't stop running, or a section of old galvanized pipe that's rusted through and started leaking inside a wall. Whatever the starting point, my job is the same — find the actual source of the problem, not just the symptom, and fix it in a way that holds up for years, not months.

I handle both repair and full installation work. That means I can patch a single failed fitting, replace a run of old pipe, or re-plumb an entire bathroom or kitchen if that's what the job calls for. A lot of the homes I work on in Libby, Troy, and the surrounding towns still have their original galvanized supply lines from decades ago. Galvanized pipe corrodes from the inside out, which means low water pressure and discolored water are often the first sign — long before a pipe actually bursts. If your water pressure has dropped over the years or you're seeing rust-colored water when you first turn on a tap, that's usually galvanized pipe on its way out, and it's worth having it looked at before it fails on its own schedule instead of yours.

I also see a fair amount of work tied to well systems on rural properties outside town — pressure tank issues, foot valves, and supply lines that freeze in exposed crawlspaces during a hard winter.
