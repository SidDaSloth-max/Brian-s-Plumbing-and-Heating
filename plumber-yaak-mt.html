Plumbing & Heating Service in the Yaak Valley

The Yaak is one of the most remote and densely forested corners of Montana — a valley carved by the Yaak River roughly 37 miles northwest of Libby, tucked up against the Purcell Mountains near the Idaho and British Columbia borders. It's unincorporated, sparsely populated, and known for exactly that remoteness: heavy timber, long gravel roads, and homes and cabins that are often well off the beaten path. Plenty of properties out here rely on private wells, septic systems, and standalone propane or wood-fired heat rather than municipal utilities.

That changes what plumbing and heating work actually looks like in the Yaak compared to in-town Libby. A lot of the calls I take out here involve well pump and pressure tank troubleshooting, supply lines that run through unheated crawlspaces or are buried shallow enough to freeze in a hard winter, and heating systems that have to work harder because a cabin sits empty for stretches at a time. I've been working on rural systems like these throughout Lincoln County for more than 30 years, and I make the trip out to the Yaak for both routine work and emergency calls.
