About Brian's Plumbing, Heating & Mechanical

A Montana-certified Master Plumber serving Libby, MT and Lincoln County.

Best Service in Lincoln County

Brian's Plumbing, Heating & Mechanical is owned and operated by a Montana-certified Master Plumber with more than 30 years of hands-on experience. There's no dispatch center, no rotating crew of subcontractors, and no upsell — when you call, you're talking directly to the person who will show up, diagnose the problem, and do the repair.

That single-plumber approach means every job gets the same standard: figure out what's actually wrong before touching anything, explain it in plain terms, and fix it in a way that holds up. It's the same approach whether the job is a five-minute faucet repair or a full furnace replacement in the middle of winter.

Specializing in Residential Plumbing & Heating

With over 30 years of experience, I specialize in residential plumbing and heating services, including emergency plumbing for urgent situations. That covers everything from routine repairs and fixture installation to full water heater replacements and furnace service — the full range of what "Plumbing, Heating & Mechanical" in the business name actually means. I guarantee that plumbing repairs are done properly the first time, so you're not calling back a month later for the same problem.

Serving Libby and Lincoln County

Most of my work is centered in Libby, MT, where I'm based, but I regularly service homes and cabins throughout Lincoln County — including Troy, the Yaak Valley, and Trego near Lake Koocanusa. The area's older housing stock, rural well systems, and hard winters mean plumbing and heating problems here often look different than they would in a bigger city, and three decades of working on exactly these kinds of systems is what lets me diagnose them quickly and fix them correctly.

5.0 Stars, 15 Reviews

Brian's Plumbing is rated 5.0 stars based on 15 customer reviews. Customers describe the work as honest, reasonable, and reliable — including being there the same night for a hot water tank leak that couldn't wait until Monday. That kind of responsiveness, and standing behind the work afterward, is what's kept people calling back and recommending the business to their neighbors for over 30 years.
