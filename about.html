Becoming a Master Plumber in Montana

Montana licenses plumbers through a tiered system that requires years of documented on-the-job training under a licensed journeyman or master plumber, followed by state examinations covering code, safety, and trade knowledge, before a Master Plumber license is issued. Reaching that level isn't a quick certification — it reflects years of supervised hands-on work across the full range of residential and light commercial plumbing before the state considers someone qualified to run jobs independently and take full responsibility for the work. That's the standard behind the "Montana Certified Master Plumber" credential you see throughout this site, along with being fully licensed, bonded, and insured for the protection of every customer.
