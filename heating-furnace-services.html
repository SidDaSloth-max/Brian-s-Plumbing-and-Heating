Furnace & Boiler Service for Lincoln County Winters

Heat isn't optional in Libby, MT. Winters here run long and cold, with stretches well below freezing that put real stress on furnaces, boilers, and the mechanical systems that keep a house livable. A furnace that's been quietly losing efficiency for a few years usually picks the worst possible night to finally quit, and that's when most of my heating calls come in — a home with no heat and a cold snap that isn't letting up.

As part of "Plumbing, Heating & Mechanical," heating work has always been a core part of what I do, not an add-on. I service both furnaces and boilers, work on gas and electric systems, and handle everything from a simple igniter replacement to swapping out an aging unit for a new one sized correctly for your house.

  • Furnace repair — igniters, thermocouples, blower motors, and control boards
  • Boiler repair and maintenance
  • Furnace and boiler installation and replacement
  • Annual heating system tune-ups and safety checks
  • Thermostat repair and replacement
  • No-heat emergency diagnosis
  • Ductwork and vent inspection related to heating performance

Signs Your Heating System Needs Attention

A furnace that's cycling on and off more frequently than it used to, taking longer to bring a house up to temperature, making a new rattling or squealing noise, or driving up your heating bill without an obvious reason are all signs worth having checked before the coldest part of winter arrives. Catching a failing heat exchanger or a worn blower motor in October is a lot less stressful — and usually cheaper — than discovering it during a January cold snap.

I provide heating and furnace service throughout Libby, Troy, Yaak, Trego, and the rest of Lincoln County. If your heat has gone out or you want a system checked over before winter sets in, call anytime — I take emergency no-heat calls around the clock.
