Plumbing & Heating Service in Troy, MT

Troy sits about 18 miles west of Libby along the Kootenai River, right where U.S. Route 2 follows the river toward the Idaho state line. It's a small town — home to roughly 800 people — but it draws a steady stream of visitors year-round for Kootenai Falls, one of the largest undammed waterfalls in Montana, just a few miles east of town along the river. Between the river-adjacent housing, the older homes near downtown, and the cabins scattered along Bull Lake to the north, Troy has a mix of plumbing and heating systems that all need slightly different care.

I've been servicing homes in Troy for as long as I've been working in Libby, and I make the drive out regularly — it's close enough that a same-day call for a leak or a dead furnace is rarely a problem. Whether you're in one of the older houses near the river or a cabin up toward Bull Lake, I handle the plumbing and heating work the same way: show up, diagnose it correctly, and fix it right.
