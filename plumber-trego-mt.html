Plumbing & Heating Service in Trego, MT

Trego sits along Montana Highway 37 — the Lake Koocanusa Scenic Byway — northeast of Libby, in the shadow of the Kootenai National Forest. It's a small, close-knit community best known locally for the Dirty Shame Saloon, and for the lake homes and vacation cabins that line the shores of Koocanusa and the nearby Fisher River drainage. A good share of the properties out here are seasonal — occupied hard in summer and left unattended through the winter — which brings its own set of plumbing challenges.

Seasonal cabins are exactly where I see the most costly plumbing failures if they're not handled correctly: a water system that isn't properly winterized before the owners leave for the season, then a hard freeze that splits a pipe no one discovers until spring thaw floods the place. I handle both the preventive side — draining and winterizing a system before you close up for the year — and the repair side when something's already gone wrong.
