Why Winter Makes Emergencies More Common Here

Lincoln County's winters are hard on plumbing. Exposed pipe in unheated crawlspaces, older homes without adequate insulation around supply lines, and cabins that sit empty for stretches during cold snaps are all common causes of frozen and burst pipes in Libby, Troy, Yaak, and Trego. If a pipe has already frozen, don't wait it out — a frozen line that isn't thawed correctly is often the one that eventually splits and floods a wall or floor. Shut off the main water valve if you suspect a burst pipe, and call right away.
