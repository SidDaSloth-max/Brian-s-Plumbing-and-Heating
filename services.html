Plumbing and Heating Work Built for Libby's Older Homes and Cold Winters

Most of the plumbing and heating calls I run in Libby, MT come down to two things: age and cold. A lot of housing stock in Lincoln County dates back to the mining and timber boom years, which means galvanized supply lines that are finally rusting through, cast iron drain lines nearing the end of their service life, and water heaters that were installed decades ago and are still limping along. On top of that, Libby sees hard winters with sustained sub-freezing stretches, which puts real stress on exposed pipe, unheated crawlspaces, and older furnaces that were never designed to run this hard for this long.

That combination is exactly what I specialize in. As a Montana-certified Master Plumber with more than 30 years in the trade, I've replaced enough galvanized pipe and diagnosed enough dead furnaces in this county to know where the problems usually hide — and how to fix them without tearing a house apart. Whether you need a same-day repair, a full water heater replacement, or a furnace tune-up before the first hard freeze, I handle it myself, start to finish.
